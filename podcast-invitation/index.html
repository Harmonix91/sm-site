Beyond the Model. Where Value is Created.

Investors used to buy infrastructure for stable yield and leave it alone. Not any more. Across infrastructure, industrial, and the services platforms underneath them, value creation is the whole game — and it isn't delivered by the model. It's delivered, quarter after quarter, by the fund-side Asset Managers, Operating Partners, and Portfolio Company Executives who turn underwriting into operating reality.

This is where they tell us how. Not theory. Real stories from the people in the room.

Who We're Looking For

Three voices, at the centre of every value creation story in PE-backed real assets. Fund-side Asset Managers, overseeing portfolio performance and managing the relationship with portco leadership. Operating Partners with the mandate to drive change across portfolios and shape the value creation playbook. Portfolio Company Executives leading value creation initiatives on the ground — ideally post-exit, so they can talk openly.

People willing to share the real stories. What the model assumed, what the operators had to do, what worked, what didn't.

The Format

A 45–60 minute conversation over a video call. Edited down to 30–40 minutes for release.

The questions are about your lens and your experience across the platforms and portfolio companies you've been involved with — not a deep dive into any one deal, and not a request for confidential specifics. We're after the patterns, the judgement, and the operating reality that only shows up once you're in the work.

Seven recurring threads. Tap any node to explore.

  1. The economics — how money gets made in these platforms, the risks the underwriting flagged that could derail long-term value, and what an LP needs to understand
  2. Operating reality — what you only really understand once you're inside the platform
  3. The levers — the most effective value creation levers you've pulled, and what made them work
  4. Stakeholder relationships — sponsor, board, customer, supply chain, regulator: the partners every value creation plan depends on
  5. The pattern across deals — what shows up every time, and what changes the most
  6. The contrarian view — what your part of the market gets wrong
  7. The handover — what you'd tell the next operator walking into a platform like the ones you've run

You control the detail level. Questions sent ahead. Edit review before it goes live.
