About the Show

Where infrastructure gets solved.

Infrastructure has stopped being a buy-and-hold game. Grids, heat, storage, generation, the platforms and the capital behind them: the returns now come from genuine value creation, from solving hard operating and commercial problems, not from the model that underwrote the deal. That work is done by the investors, operators and platform leaders who turn a thesis into something that actually functions.

This is where they talk about how. Not the mission, the mechanics. Real stories from the people solving the infrastructure puzzle once the capital is committed.

The Format

The Format

An episode runs around 30 to 40 minutes, recorded over a video call.

The questions are about your lens and your experience across the platforms and portfolio companies you've been involved with, not a deep dive into any one deal, and not a request for confidential specifics. We're after the patterns, the judgement, and the operating reality that only shows up once you're in the work.

You control the detail level. Questions sent ahead. Edit review before it goes live.

A handful of recurring threads we'll work through, all pointed at how you actually solve it:

  1. The economics. How money gets made, the risks the underwriting flagged, and what an investor would need to know to back it
  2. Operating reality. What you only really understand once you're inside the platform
  3. The levers. The most effective value creation levers you've pulled, and what made them work
  4. Stakeholder relationships. Sponsor, board, customer, supply chain, regulator: the partners every value creation plan depends on
  5. The pattern across deals. What shows up every time, and what changes the most
  6. The contrarian view. What your part of the market over-indexes on or undervalues
  7. The handover. What you'd tell the next person stepping into a mandate like the ones you've run
