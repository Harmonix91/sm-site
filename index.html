How We Work

A search, run like diligence.

We don't put a leader in front of you without underwriting them: a defined process, weekly steering, and written evidence at every stage. Every search runs through a client data room, so you work from the research as it is produced rather than from a summary of it at the end. Four to six weeks, briefing to presented longlist. Weeks 1–2
Research

A full map of the market, not a database search. Who runs the relevant businesses, who has scaled one before, and who is reachable. Adjacent markets included deliberately, because that is where the under-the-radar candidates sit.

Weeks 2–4
Engagement

A structured multi-channel approach to leaders who are not looking. Weekly steering calls keep you inside the search, not waiting on it.

Weeks 4–6
Assessment

A scored competency interview against five or six competencies agreed with you at briefing, written up for every candidate we present. Evidence, not gut feel.

Week 6
Longlist

A longlist of assessed candidates with comparative evidence behind every name. You confirm the shortlist from it and interview with the full picture, then we run the offer to acceptance.

To Day 100
Landing

Onboarding and a first 100 days plan, built with you. The mandate isn't done at the offer. It's done when the plan is moving. Six month guarantee.

Markets

Working across Industrial Platforms, delivering business-critical outcomes.

Power & Renewables

Solar, onshore and offshore wind, battery storage and flexible generation, energy from waste, bioenergy and hydrogen. Merchant exposure, route to market and the pace of build. The businesses that win are led by teams who can price risk and still move first.

Utilities & Grid

Transmission and distribution, heat networks, water, waste and environmental services. Regulated returns and long-dated contracts reward operational discipline. The best teams sweat the asset base, hold the compliance line, and still find the growth.

Transport & Aviation

Airports and aviation services, ports, rail, road and logistics infrastructure. Long concession lives, heavy capital programmes, and a service that has to run every day regardless. Leadership here is judged on delivery under public scrutiny as much as on margin.

Digital Infrastructure

Data centres, fibre and towers. Demand is compounding faster than capacity can be built. Leadership decides who delivers at pace without dropping availability, and who is still integrating the last acquisition when the next one lands.

Industrial & Technical Services

Manufacturing and engineering groups, operations and maintenance providers, EPC and specialist contractors, technical advisory and facilities management. No balance sheet full of assets, and exactly the same problem: margin, utilisation, contract renewal, and someone capable of running it well.
