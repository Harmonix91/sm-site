How We Work

A search, run like diligence.

You wouldn't commit capital to an asset without underwriting it. We don't put a leader in front of you without the same discipline: a defined process, weekly steering, and evidence at every stage. Six weeks, mandate to shortlist. An accepted offer around day sixty.

Where We Focus
